# CroweTrade Autonomous Trading Authorization and Risk Acknowledgment

DRAFT v1 (2026-08-08). Template for counsel review before any live-capital
signature. The SHA-256 of the final text becomes `waiverSha256` in every signed
policy envelope, binding this exact language into the consent artifact.

---

I am authorizing CroweTrade, a software product of Crowe Logic, Inc., to
execute cryptocurrency transactions autonomously on my behalf, using my
wallet, under the limits I have set in the attached policy envelope.

I understand and agree to each of the following:

1. My funds, my keys. CroweTrade is non-custodial software. Crowe Logic never
   holds keys that can withdraw my funds. The trading key operates only within
   the caps, program allowlist, and expiry I am signing.

2. Autonomy. Once armed, transactions execute without my per-trade approval,
   at machine speed, including while I am asleep or offline. My consent is to
   the policy, not to each trade.

3. Finality. Blockchain transactions are irreversible. The veto window lets me
   instruct an immediate market unwind of a recent entry; it is a second trade
   at the then-current price, not a cancellation of the first.

4. Risk of total loss. The assets this system trades are among the most
   volatile and adversarial instruments in existence. Safety screening reduces
   avoidable losses; it cannot eliminate loss, and I may lose everything I
   allocate. I am allocating only what I can afford to lose entirely.

5. No advice, no guarantee. CroweTrade is a tool executing my policy. Nothing
   in it is investment advice, and past results, simulated or live, do not
   predict future results.

6. My controls. I may tighten limits or kill trading instantly at any time.
   Loosening limits takes effect only after the arming delay and a fresh
   signature. Revoking the envelope halts all future transactions.

7. Simulated records. Results from the paper phase are simulations. They
   include modeled slippage and fees but did not move real capital, and live
   results will differ.

Signed by wallet signature over the policy envelope carrying this document's
hash.
